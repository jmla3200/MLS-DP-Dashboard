Key Insight:
There is a weak correlation between salary investment and goal output among Designated Players. Several high-earning players follow uniform output relative to their cost, while some lower-paid players deliver exceptional value with higher goal contributions per dollar spent.
Methodology Note:
These metrics favor attacking players (forwards and attacking midfielders) as they focus on goals and assists. However, most clubs strategically allocate their Designated Player spots to these positions, making this analysis particularly relevant for evaluating DP value and investment efficiency.

Players are categorized based on whether their cost per contribution is below (more efficient), around (expected efficiency), or above (less efficient) a median or threshold value. This highlights players who provide their contributions at a lower cost, regardless of their total compensation, by normalizing their output against their salary.
